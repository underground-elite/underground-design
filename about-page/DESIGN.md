## Brand & Style

This design system channels the sun-soaked, coastal luxury of the Mediterranean coastline—juxtaposing hand-painted majolica ceramics with sun-drenched lemon groves and crystalline coastal waters. The visual experience evokes warmth, effortless European elegance, vibrancy, and polished leisure. It rejects sterile enterprise grays in favor of rich maritime navies, luminous ivory bases, and optimistic citrus touches.

### Style Direction
- **Tactile Ceramic & Soft Glassmorphism:** Surfaces emulate high-glaze ceramic tiles, honed limestone, and coastal sea glass. Crisp, low-opacity cobalt and gold hairline borders frame frosted, translucent layers floating above soft sunlit canvases.
- **Editorial Coastal Luxury:** Generous whitespace, refined typographic rhythm, and architectural proportions balance bold, saturated citrus accents with deep, authoritative navy letterforms.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Pure whites interact with buttery cream undertones and delicate azure ambient shadows, creating an inviting, daylight-rich tactile presence.

## Colors

The palette balances energetic solar tones with coastal aquatic depths and warm mineral foundations.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Citrus Zest (`#FFA62B`):** A warm, golden-amber citrus tone reserved for primary calls to action, high-energy highlights, interactive highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary — Amalfi Tile (`#2E5AA7`):** A deep, saturated Mediterranean majolica blue providing structural stability, key interactive elements, badges, and prominent icons.
- **Tertiary — Sea Breeze (`#86C5FF`):** An airy, luminous sky blue deployed for tinted container fills, soft focus rings, gentle pill tags, and secondary action highlights.
- **Soft Accent — Cream Gelato (`#F8E6A0`):** A gentle custard yellow used for subtle highlight washes, callout containers, warm badges, and delicate divider accents.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Linen (`#FFFDF8`) and Ceramic White (`#FAFAFA`) create a sun-bleached, luminous light-mode environment.
  - Surface Elevation: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with 75%–90% opacity over frosted glass layers.
- **Typography & High-Contrast Neutral:**
  - Ink Primary: Deep Mediterranean Navy (`#0F1E36`) offers crisp, high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure black.
  - Ink Secondary: Amalfi Navy (`#1A2E4D`) and Muted Slate Blue (`#4A5D78`) for descriptive prose and metadata.
- **Hairlines & Borders:** Semi-transparent Sea Breeze (`rgba(134, 197, 255, 0.35)`) and delicate Golden Ochre (`rgba(255, 166, 43, 0.25)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, editorial energy of **Epilogue** with the humanist, approachable clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue):** Provides architectural confidence reminiscent of classic Mediterranean resort signage and Italian editorial typography. Generous tracking on all-caps subheads counterbalances tight, confident letterspacing on large display titles.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers fluid reading comfort across dense specifications, booking interfaces, and microcopy. Its open counters remain crisp against bright ivory and frosted tile backgrounds.
- **Rendering & Coloration:** Headings are rendered exclusively in Deep Mediterranean Navy (`#0F1E36`). Body paragraphs take `#1A2E4D` for primary reading and Muted Slate Blue (`#4A5D78`) for contextual support.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an airy, structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to celebrate high-impact imagery, tile-like data cards, and breezy spatial breaks.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer page canvas `margin: 3rem`. Content bounds cap comfortably at a maximum width of `1360px` to maintain focused line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `gutter: 1.25rem` and outer `margin-tablet: 2rem`. Multicolumn tile grids reflow gracefully to dual-column stacks.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Cards expand to full-width containers with horizontal scrolling carousels for chip navigation and tile collections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth emulates smooth glazed ceramics, frosted Italian glass, and coastal sunshine casting soft, azure-tinted ambient shadows rather than muddy monochromatic drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Scale
- **Flat Ground (Level 0):** Used for foundational canvas backgrounds (`#FFFDF8`).
- **Ceramic Card (Level 1):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(46, 90, 167, 0.07)`. Crisp, sunlit elevation suited for resting cards, input containers, and structural sections.
- **Floating Overlay (Level 2):** `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(46, 90, 167, 0.06)`. Used for hover states, dropdown menus, and popovers.
- **Modal & Coastal Glass (Level 3):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.16), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 166, 43, 0.08)`. Applied to priority dialogs, navigation rails, and prominent drawers.

### Frosted Sea-Glass Treatment
Elevated surface elements leverage a backdrop filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(160%)` overlaid on `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` and finished with a subtle perimeter hairline: `border: 1px solid rgba(134, 197, 255, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level 2 to reflect smoothed sea glass, polished ceramic coasters, and architectural Italian arches.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Buttons, input fields, dropdown toggles, and notification toasts.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Content cards, dialog boxes, and floating panel containers.
- **Hero Containers & Feature Tiles (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Primary showcase blocks, media containers, and modal views.
- **Pill Exception (`9999px`):** Status indicators, active category filter chips, and circular icon buttons preserve pure pill curvature to contrast against structured rectangular tile grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Citrus Zest):** Solid fill `#FFA62B` with Deep Navy text `#0F1E36`, font weight 600. Hover transitions to `#F29618` with subtle golden glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(255, 166, 43, 0.38)`).
- **Secondary (Amalfi Tile):** Solid fill `#2E5AA7` with pure white text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#244887`.
- **Tertiary / Frosted Blue:** Tinted background `rgba(134, 197, 255, 0.20)` with border `1px solid rgba(46, 90, 167, 0.24)` and text `#2E5AA7`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Frosted pure white background with hairline border `rgba(74, 93, 120, 0.25)` and text `#4A5D78`. Full pill radius (`roundedness: 9999px`).
- **Selected State:** Cream Gelato background `#F8E6A0` with border `1px solid #FFA62B` and bold navy text `#0F1E36`.
- **Badge Variant:** Mini tags featuring vibrant Sea Breeze tint (`#E8F4FF`) with navy Amalfi microcopy.

### Cards & Tile Panels
- **Glazed Ceramic Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with Level 1 elevation, `1rem` radius, and border `1px solid rgba(134, 197, 255, 0.22)`.
- **Sunlit Feature Card:** Cream Gelato tint wash (`rgba(248, 230, 160, 0.18)`), framed by a golden hairline border (`rgba(255, 166, 43, 0.3)`), ideal for special notices or premium itineraries.

### Form Inputs
- **Base State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid rgba(74, 93, 120, 0.25)`, text `#0F1E36`, placeholder `#4A5D78` at 60% opacity. Corner radius `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#2E5AA7` paired with an outer aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(134, 197, 255, 0.35)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** Pure white fill with `1.5px solid #4A5D78` border.
- **Checked:** Solid Amalfi Tile fill `#2E5AA7` featuring a crisp white checkmark icon or inner dot, supported by a subtle Sea Breeze outer halo on focus.

### Lists & Dividers
- List items feature generous padding (`space-md`) with separating borders rendered as delicate coastal hairlines (`rgba(46, 90, 167, 0.08)`).
- Interactive list tiles exhibit an active hover state shifting the background to `rgba(248, 230, 160, 0.15)` with an accent bar in Citrus Zest (`#FFA62B`).
